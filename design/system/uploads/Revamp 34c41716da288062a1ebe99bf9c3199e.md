## 1. Vision & positioning

**Hero one-liner (locked):**

> Hi, I'm Tim Yeo. I help quiet achievers have impact at work — without pretending to be an extrovert.
> 

**Sub-headline (locked):**

1:1 coaching, a book, and a school of practical tiny habits — built on 20+ years as a designer and design leader in tech, and over a thousand coaching conversations with introverts since 2021.

**Opinionated thesis line:**

> Just because you are quiet doesn't mean you have nothing to say.
> 

**Core belief** (lifted from the coaching proposal):

- The things you need to have impact at work are *skills*.
- Skills can be practised.
- With practise, skills improve.
- Confidence grows.
- Impact and influence increases.
- Who you are, your identity, shouldn't limit you (unless you let it).

**Primary thesis:** Sell **Tim Yeo the coach** first. The rest of the ecosystem (book, school, podcast, newsletter, playbooks, Toni) are on-ramps to coaching.

**Pricing ladder (top → bottom):**

- 1:1 Coaching plans — Sprint $2k · Accelerator $4k · Level-Up $6k
- Deep Dive workshop — $300 (one 90-min session, or two 45-min sessions)
- Book (low-ticket)
- School courses
- Newsletter / playbooks / podcast / Toni (free, top-of-funnel)

**Domain decision:** stay on `thequietachievr.com`. Don't split to `timyeo.com` — SEO authority is too valuable. *The Quiet Achiever* can mean the brand, the book, *and* the man.
